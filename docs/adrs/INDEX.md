## Read this first

[ADR-2608010960](ADR-2608010960-adoption-gates-precede-the-port.md) constrains every other
ADR in this ledger. Five ADRs authorize a Rust workspace; that one says **do not build it
yet**. The decisions here establish *correctness* — derivability, round-trip fidelity,
authorization recall, merge integrity, parser divergence, all measured. None of them
establishes the system is *worth using*, and the two assumptions that would are untested.

## Evidence conventions

Claims in these ADRs are labelled **measured** or **untested**. Measured claims cite an
experiment in [FINDINGS.md](../../FINDINGS.md) with a pre-registered kill criterion from
[PROTOTYPE.md](../../PROTOTYPE.md). Estimates are labelled as estimates. Nothing in this
ledger asserts a performance number that was not observed.
